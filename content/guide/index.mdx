Moonbase is a self-hosted storefront and license-delivery system for digital products. You run two parts on your own server: the **API** (with MongoDB) that handles licenses, payments and downloads, and the **app**, the storefront and dashboard your customers and staff use. Customers pay with Stripe, PayPal or Coinbase Commerce and get their license and downloads at once.

This guide is for store owners: installing Moonbase, keeping it running, and using every feature from the dashboard. Building loaders and integrations is covered in the [API documentation](/docs/api), and extending Moonbase in the [plugin documentation](/docs/plugin).

<Callout type="info" title="Moonbase 2.0">
  This guide covers Moonbase 2.0.0. Coming from 1.8? Start with [Upgrading from 1.8](/docs/guide/upgrading).
</Callout>

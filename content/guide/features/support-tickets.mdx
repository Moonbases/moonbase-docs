## How customers open a ticket

Customers open **Support** in their dashboard and click **New ticket**:

- **Subject** and **Message**.
- **Order** (optional): one of their orders, or "Not about an order". The **Get help** link next to an order in **Purchases** opens a ticket about it directly.

They follow the conversation on the same page and can **Close ticket** when their question is answered; replying later opens it again. Messages are text only; there are no attachments.

Each customer can have up to five tickets open at once.

## Answer tickets

**Dashboard → Tickets** (under **Customers**) is your inbox. Admins need the **Support** area: give it to the admins who handle support (see [Admin permissions](/docs/guide/security#admin-roles-and-permissions)). The sidebar shows how many tickets are waiting for an answer.

- Tabs: **Open** (waiting for you), **Answered** (waiting for the customer), **Closed** and **All**.
- Search by ticket number, subject or customer.
- Next to each conversation you see the customer, their recent orders and the order the ticket is about.

Your replies appear to the customer under the store's name. You can close a ticket, or **Reopen** a closed one.

## Email notifications

With email configured:

- New tickets and customer replies are emailed to the **Support email** in **Store settings → General**. Set it, or nobody is notified.
- Your replies are emailed to the customer.

<Callout type="info" title="Keep secrets out of tickets">
  Ask customers never to paste passwords, license keys or payment details into a ticket.
</Callout>

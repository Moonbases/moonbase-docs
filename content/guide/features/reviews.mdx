---
title: Reviews
description: Let verified owners rate and review your products, and moderate and reply from the dashboard.
---

## Turn reviews on or off

In **Store settings → General → Product reviews**:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | No reviews, and no Reviews section on product pages. |
| **Publish right away** (default) | New reviews appear on the product page immediately. |
| **Approve before publishing** | New and edited reviews wait for you in **Dashboard → Reviews**. |

## Who can review

Only customers who own a product, through a completed purchase or a redeemed license, can review it. Each customer writes one review per product: a rating from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional title and text. They can edit or delete it later.

Product pages show the average rating, the number of reviews and the star distribution, with every review marked **Verified owner**. Visitors can sort by newest, highest or lowest rating. Product cards in the shop show the stars too.

## Moderate and reply

**Dashboard → Reviews** (under **Catalog**) lists every review with its rating, product, customer, date and status. Admins need the **Products** area. The sidebar shows how many reviews are awaiting approval.

Filter by status (**Published**, **Awaiting approval**, **Hidden**) or by rating, then:

- **Publish** or **Hide** a review.
- Write a **Public reply**: it appears under the review as "Reply from" your store. Each review has one reply, which you can update or remove.
- **Delete review**: it disappears for good, and the customer can write a new one while they own the product.

When a refund or dispute removes a customer's access, their review is hidden automatically ("Hidden (refunded)").

To be told about new reviews in Discord or your own systems, subscribe a [webhook](/docs/guide/features/webhooks) to `review.created`.

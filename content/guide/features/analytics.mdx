## Analytics

**Dashboard → Analytics** shows how the store is doing. Admins need the **Orders** area.

Pick a period: **7 days**, **30 days** (default), **90 days** or **12 months**. Each number is compared with the period before it.

- **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Average order** and **New customers**.
- A revenue chart per day (per month for 12 months), in your browser's time zone.
- **Top products**, **Payment methods** and **Discount codes** (orders, amount given away and revenue for each code).
- **More numbers**: customers who paid, discounts given, refunded or disputed orders, and, when you use them, checkout reminders sent and the revenue recovered by them.

How it is counted:

- Only completed orders count. Revenue excludes tax, and partial refunds are subtracted.
- Free orders don't count toward the average order.
- Only orders in the current store currency are added up. Orders in an earlier currency are listed separately.

## Dashboard overview

The admin overview (**Dashboard → Overview**) shows at a glance:

- an update notice when a new Moonbase version is out (owner only);
- the **setup guide** (see below);
- a warning when orders failed to fulfill;
- revenue, orders, average order and new customers for the last 30 days, with a revenue chart (Orders area);
- all-time totals: revenue, customers, active licenses, products and files;
- recent orders and top products.

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> anywhere in the dashboard to jump to a page.

### Setup guide

New stores see **Get your store ready** on the overview, with the steps you can act on:

1. **Add your first product**: name, price and a cover image are enough to start selling.
2. **Make it yours**: upload a logo and pick the brand color (owner).
3. **Upload a download**: buyers get it in their dashboard right after paying.
4. **Tell buyers how to reach you**: a support email or Discord invite (owner).

It disappears once every step is done, or when you close it.

Customers sign in to the store and open their dashboard from the user menu. Their sidebar shows only what applies to them.

| Page | What customers do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | See their products with expiry dates, open each product's **Downloads**, and **Redeem a license** key. |
| **Purchases** | See their orders, invoices, refunds and credit notes, and **Get help** about an order. |
| **Subscriptions** | Cancel or resume renewal, change plan, update their card. See [Subscriptions](/docs/guide/features/subscriptions). |
| **Teams** | Share seats with teammates. Shown only to customers who bought a product sold per seat. |
| **Creator earnings** | Their creator codes, share links and commissions. Shown only to [creators](/docs/guide/features/discounts-and-creators#creator-codes). |
| **Wholesale** | Buy and hand out keys. Shown only to [resellers](/docs/guide/features/resellers). |
| **Support** | Open and follow [support tickets](/docs/guide/features/support-tickets). |
| **Account** | Profile, password, PIN, store credit, billing details, devices and networks. |

## Account

- **Profile**: email, account type, member since and Discord link status. Customers can **Change username** (with their password).
- **Password**: changing it signs them out on every device. With email configured, a forgotten password can be reset from the sign-in page.
- **Account PIN**: a secret used to link their Discord account with the store's Discord bot (`/link`) and to confirm sensitive actions. It is shown only once.
- **Store credit**: balance, history, and **Redeem a gift card**. See [Gift cards and store credit](/docs/guide/features/gift-cards-and-credit).
- **Billing details**: name or company, VAT or tax ID, address and country, printed on their invoices.

Email addresses can't be changed. Customers can't delete their account themselves; you can, from **Dashboard → Users** (see [Customers](/docs/guide/features/customers)).

## Devices and networks

Your products lock each account to a limited number of IP addresses and devices (see [IP and device limits](/docs/guide/security#ip-and-device-limits)). In **Account → Devices and networks**, customers see how many they use and can:

- **Reset** their IP addresses or devices, to bind new ones the next time they use your software.
- **Rename device** (e.g. "Gaming PC") and **Remove device** one at a time, with when each was last used.
- See **Products with their own devices**, and **Remove device** or, for floating licenses, **Sign out device** to free a place right away.

Resets and removals need the customer's password and follow **Store settings → Security → Self-service reset cooldown** (24 hours by default; signing out a floating device has no cooldown). A device that went over the account's limit (for example when seats ran out) shows "Over the device limit · can't sign in" until the customer removes another one.

## Teams

Customers who bought several seats of a product sold per seat share them in **Teams**:

1. Enter a teammate's **username or email** (an existing account) and click **Add**. The teammate gets an email and the product appears in their own account.
2. The buyer keeps one seat, so they can add one teammate fewer than their seats. "Every seat is taken" means they need more seats.
3. **Remove** takes a teammate's access away at once. Teammates can **Leave** a team from **Teams you're on**.

An account can be on one team per product. When seats lapse or are refunded, the most recently added teammates lose access first ("No seat").

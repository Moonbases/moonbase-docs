## Invoices

Every paid order gets an invoice with a sequential number, such as `INV-000042`. Free orders and orders paid entirely with store credit don't get one. Renewals and paid plan switches get their own.

Set what invoices show in **Store settings → Billing → Invoices**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Legal name** | Your business name. The store name is used when empty. |
| **VAT or tax ID** | Printed under your name. |
| **Business address** | Printed under your name. |
| **Billing email** | Contact address on invoices. The support email is used when empty. |
| **Invoice number prefix** | `INV-` by default. Numbers continue from the last invoice, without gaps. |
| **Invoice footer** | Registration details, payment terms or a thank-you note. |
| **Credit note prefix** | `CN-` by default. Credit notes have their own numbers. |

Invoices always show your **current** billing settings, so fill them in before you start selling.

The buyer's details come from the card checkout (with Stripe Tax on, Stripe collects the billing address) or from the billing details in their account.

### Where to find them

- **Customers**: **Dashboard → Purchases** has an **Invoice** link under each paid order. The receipt email links it too.
- **Admins** with the **Orders** area: open the order in **Dashboard → Orders**.

Invoices open as a printable page with **Print or save as PDF**.

## Credit notes

Invoices are never changed. When an invoiced order is refunded, it gets a credit note instead, with its own number that references the invoice:

- A **full refund** at Stripe or PayPal, or **Revoke access** on the order, issues a credit note for the whole amount (or what is left after partial refunds).
- Each **partial refund** at Stripe or PayPal issues a credit note for the refunded amount, with tax credited in proportion. The order and its access stay.
- Disputes don't issue credit notes.

Customers find credit notes next to the invoice in **Dashboard → Purchases**.

## Stripe Tax

Stripe Tax calculates VAT or sales tax at **card** checkout. Set up your tax registrations in the Stripe Dashboard first, then open **Store settings → Billing → Tax**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Calculate tax with Stripe Tax** | Checkout asks for the buyer's address and adds VAT or sales tax where you're registered. |
| **Prices include tax** | Tax is taken from your prices (common in the EU) instead of added on top. Only applies with Stripe Tax on. |
| **Collect VAT IDs** | Business buyers can enter a VAT or tax ID at card checkout; it's printed on their invoice. |

Tax is stored on each order and shown on its invoice. Revenue in [Analytics](/docs/guide/features/analytics) excludes tax.

<Callout type="warn" title="What Stripe Tax doesn't cover">
  - PayPal and crypto orders never get tax.
  - While Stripe Tax is on, **store credit can't pay for any order**, whatever the payment method: Stripe would compute tax after the credit. Customers keep their balance and can use it again if you turn Stripe Tax off.
</Callout>
